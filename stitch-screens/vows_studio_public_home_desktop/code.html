THE ATELIER & OUR PHILOSOPHY

Reverence, Natural Light, and Honest Emotion

Led by founder and cinematographer Reuben Serrao across Mangalore and Goa, VOWS Studio was established to preserve Christian matrimony through the lens of quiet artistic discipline.

We reject manufactured trends, frenetic cuts, and loud wedding theatrics. Our craft is rooted in reverence, natural light, liturgical stillness, and honest emotion. Every solemnization is approached with the architectural respect due to centuries-old stone chapels and the quiet dignity of sacred covenants.

With over 8 years of Christian wedding documentation, we safeguard each legacy with strict client privacy protocols and triple-redundant magnetic LTO-8 tape cold storage.
